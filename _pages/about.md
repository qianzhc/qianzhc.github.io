Welcome to my academic website. 

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of
Public Administration at Southwest Jiaotong University in Chengdu, China. My 
current research focuses on the policy process, comparative social policy, health
policy, and poverty alleviation policy.

Before beginning my current position, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the
University of Hong Kong. I earned my Ph.D. in Social Work and Social
Administration from the University of Hong Kong in 2025. I also hold a Master of 
Management in Social Security from Renmin University of China and a Bachelor of 
Law in Social Work from Nankai University. During my doctoral studies, I was a 
visiting Ph.D. student at Rutgers University, New Brunswick.

You can contact me at
[qianzhc@swjtu.edu.cn](mailto:qianzhc@swjtu.edu.cn) with questions about my
research or opportunities for collaboration.


欢迎来到我的主页。

我是西南交通大学公共管理学院助理教授，研究主要关注政策过程、比较社会政策、卫生政策与反贫困政策等领域。

加入西南交通大学之前，我曾在香港大学从事博士后研究。我于2025年在香港大学社会工作及社会行政学系获得博士学位。此前，我先后获得南开大学社会工作专业学士学位和中国人民大学社会保障专业硕士学位。读博期间，我曾赴美国罗格斯大学新布朗斯维克分校访学。

如对我的研究感兴趣，或希望探讨合作机会，欢迎通过 [qianzhc@swjtu.edu.cn](mailto:qianzhc@swjtu.edu.cn) 与我联系。
